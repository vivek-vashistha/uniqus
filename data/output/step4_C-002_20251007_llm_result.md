# Step 4

Step 4: Allocate the transaction price to the performance obligations in the contract

The transaction price is allocated to each performance obligation - generally each distinct good or service - to depict the amount of consideration to which an entity expects to be entitled in exchange for transferring the promised goods or services to the customer.

An entity generally allocates the transaction price to each performance obligation in proportion to its stand-alone selling price. However, when specified criteria are met, a discount or variable consideration is allocated to one or more, but not all, performance obligations.

This step of the revenue model comprises two sub-steps that an entity performs at contract inception.

1. Determine stand-alone selling prices

2. Allocate the transaction price

Step 4: Allocate the transaction price to the performance obligations in the contract

## 1

**Question:** Allocation of transaction price required? 

**Answer:** Yes. Allocation of transaction price is required because the contract comprises multiple performance obligations (e.g., support services, licenses, programs, etc.), each with separately identified prices and services to be performed over the contract term. The transaction price must be allocated to each distinct performance obligation (support services for unlicensed inbound calls, chat activities, policy support, etc.) according to ASC 606 guidance.

#### Schedule of Standalone Selling Prices and Allocation

- The contract breaks down pricing for various support services and licenses (e.g., Malkom.ai, DSITE, Bridge, DSOP, IA) annually, invoiced either upfront or in monthly installments. Each service's standalone selling price is either explicitly stated or implied by the contract for each contract year.

Example (Malkom.ai):

| Transformation Service        | Year 1   | Year 2   | Year 3   | Year 4   | Year 5   |
|------------------------------|----------|----------|----------|----------|----------|
| Malkom.ai Support Services   | $85,116  | $158,419 | $170,237 | $166,369 | $158,886 |
| Malkom.ai Licences           | $702,118 | $635,783 | $604,596 | $598,385 | $592,774 |

- Other support services (DSOP, IA, DSiTE, Bridge, Program Management) are similarly disclosed with separate pricing per year.
- Where FTE-based rates are used (e.g., “USD 122 per FTE per month” for Malkom.ai), the allocation is systematic over the contract term.
- Allocations follow the proportional method based on standalone selling prices where the contract provides separate prices per obligation.

**Allocation of variable consideration**

**Question:** Is there an allocation of a variable consideration ? (ASC 606-10-32-39 to 32-41)?

**Answer:** Yes. There is allocation of variable consideration. The contract sets out scenarios where pricing is adjusted based on service volumes (e.g., FTE headcounts, booking volumes) or changes via the Change Control Procedure. Credits and additional charges linked to volume fluctuations are explicitly stated (e.g., Reduced Resource Credits or Additional Resource Charges calculated based on deviations from baseline volumes), which indicates the presence and required allocation of variable consideration to relevant obligations in line with ASC 606 guidance.
