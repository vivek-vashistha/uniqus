# Step 4: Allocate the transaction price to the performance obligations in the contract

1

**Question:** Allocation of transaction price required? 

**Answer:** **Yes.** The contract includes at least two performance obligations identified in Step 2: (a) an integrated, series performance obligation comprising the Operational Services (with the enabling Transformation/Technology services and set‑up activities bundled with them), and (b) a distinct Termination/Expiration Assistance obligation. The scope clause expressly includes Termination/Expiration Assistance alongside Operational Services and other services, evidencing more than one promise within scope, which triggers allocation under ASC 606 (relative SSP unless specific-variable consideration guidance applies). See scope of services including “Operational Services” and “Termination/Expiration Assistance” in Clause 4.1.7–4.1.8  and the detailed Termination/Expiration Assistance obligations and pricing mechanics in Clauses 43 and Schedule 5 (Termination Assistance Fee)  .

Notes supporting SSP and allocation:
- Operational Services FTE Rates by site/process and the derivation of monthly rates are specified (Schedule 5 §§4.1–4.2) . Transformation/Technology Recurring “Prices” and payment timing are set out in Appendices 5‑4/5‑5 (e.g., Malkom.ai Support/Licences; IA support/licence) , and One‑Time Charges become “Earned” upon milestone acceptance (Schedule 5 §2.2; Appendix 5‑1 example)  .  
- Termination/Expiration Assistance is a contractual promise with its own fee logic: no additional fee if performed with existing paid resources, otherwise a Termination Assistance Fee calculated from incremental FTEs at the applicable pro‑rated FTE Rate plus any agreed resource fees (Clause 43.1.5; Schedule 5 §6.3)  . Invoices for the Termination Assistance Fee and post‑termination costs are issued upon completion (or as agreed) and paid within 45 days (Schedule 5 §9.5) .

**Allocation of variable consideration**

**Question:** Is there an allocation of a variable consideration ? (ASC 606-10-32-39 to 32-41)?

**Answer:** **Yes.** Variable consideration is allocated to the performance obligation(s) to which it specifically relates:
- Allocate to the integrated series (Operational Services): 
  - Service Level Credits reduce the consideration for the month’s services and are capped by the “At Risk Amount” of 10% of Monthly Fees (Schedule 4 and Clause 12.10 illustration)  .  
  - Productivity Guarantee true‑ups (Corrected Amount/Ad Hoc Corrected Amount) are determined by comparing actual vs. adjusted Operational Service Charges across “all Statements of Work and Implementation Agreements” and settle within 60 days, clearly relating to the Operational Services series (Clause 22.4.3) .  
  - Volume‑based adjustments (ARC/RRC) are defined within the pricing schedule for Operational Services and therefore relate to the series (definitions referencing Schedule 5; Operational Services pricing framework in Schedule 5)  .  
  - Any gain‑share style one‑time benefit tied to realized savings arises from the service delivery solution and, when included, relates to the ongoing services series (see Transformation/Operational pricing construct and adjustments under Schedule 5) .  
- Allocate to Termination/Expiration Assistance:  
  - The Termination Assistance Fee is computed from incremental FTEs and agreed resources for ramp‑down/transition activities and is payable only if such additional assistance is required; as such, it relates specifically and solely to the Termination/Expiration Assistance PO and is allocated entirely to that PO when incurred (Schedule 5 §6.3; Clause 43.1.5; Schedule 5 §9.5)   .

Additional considerations:
- The Upfront Monetisation Sum and its non‑interest‑bearing repayment schedule are a financing arrangement separate from consideration for services (Clauses 3.3.1–3.3.6); they are not included in the ASC 606 transaction price allocation for revenue  .  
- Invoicing/payment timing for service fees occurs within ~45 days after the Invoice Month (Schedule 5 §§9.2–9.4), supporting the revenue streams’ use of the practical expedient for significant financing components and not affecting allocation between POs  .

Summary of allocation approach:
- Use relative SSP to allocate all fixed consideration to the integrated series PO at inception, because observable SSPs exist for its components (FTE Rates, Recurring Service “Prices,” and Earned One‑Time Charges)   .  
- Apply ASC 606 specific‑allocation guidance for variable consideration that relates specifically to (and is priced from) a particular PO (e.g., Termination Assistance Fee to the Termination/Expiration Assistance PO) and allocate routine operational variability (service credits, productivity, volume) to the series PO to best depict the consideration for those services (Contract references: Schedule 4; Clause 22.4.3; Schedule 5 §6.3)   .
