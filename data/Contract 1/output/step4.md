# **Step 4 – Allocate the Transaction Price to the Performance Obligations**

The total contract value (transaction price) under **Work Order 01** is approximately **USD 122.0 million** over five years (Exhibit A – Tab 2 Charges Summary).
This amount includes fixed service charges, rate-card-based FTE fees, technology and transformation charges, and a signing bonus offset.
Each distinct performance obligation identified in **Step 2** (Phone Support, Chat Support, PSO Back-office, and Transition Services) receives an allocation based on its stand-alone selling price (SSP).

---

### **Question:** Allocation of transaction price required?

**Answer:** **Yes.**
Rationale – Multiple distinct performance obligations exist (Phone Support, Chat Support, Policy Support Operations (PSO), and Transition/Transformation Services). ASC 606 requires the total consideration to be allocated in proportion to their relative stand-alone selling prices derived from Exhibit A (Tab 2 A & B) which separately lists annual pricing for each service line and location (India PSO/Chat, Philippines Phone, South Africa Phone, Costa Rica Phone, and rate-card shared services).

### **Allocation of Variable Consideration**

**Question:** Is there an allocation of a variable consideration (ASC 606-10-32-39 to 32-41)?
**Answer:** **Yes.**
Rationale – Variable consideration (Service Credits and Earnbacks) relates specifically to the ongoing **operational service obligations** (Phone, Chat, and PSO) tied to Service Level Agreements in **Exhibit B**.
These adjustments do **not** apply to Transition or Transformation milestones. Therefore, variable consideration is allocated **only to the service performance obligations** that are subject to SLA metrics, consistent with ASC 606-10-32-40(a) and (b).

---

✅ **Summary Conclusion**

> The transaction price is allocated across performance obligations based on their relative stand-alone selling prices extracted from Exhibit A (Tab 2 A & B) of the Work Order.
> Variable consideration (service credits and earnbacks) is allocated exclusively to the ongoing service-delivery obligations (Phone, Chat, and PSO).
> Transition and Transformation fees are recognized separately at the time related milestones are achieved.
