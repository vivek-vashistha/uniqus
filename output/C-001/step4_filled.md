**Question:** Allocation of transaction price required? 

**Answer:** **Yes** — The contract contains three distinct series performance obligations: (1) Unlicensed inbound Phone support, (2) Unlicensed Chat support, and (3) PSO back-office processing (identified in Step 2). The Annexure to Exhibit A provides observable prices by service stream (“Fixed Service Charges” by service line and location), which serve as stand-alone selling prices (SSP) for each performance obligation. Therefore, allocation on a relative SSP basis is required. The committed transaction price used for allocation equals total committed charges for the term, net of the consideration payable to the customer (signing bonus) and the DTI pass-through, and excluding optional rate-card services that are billed only when deployed:
- Fixed Service Charges (PSO, Phone, Chat): $109,921,358 (Tab 2A by service line/location)  
- Plus Technology Charges over the term: $3,732,964 
- Plus Transition Charges: $5,250,000; Plus Transformation Charges: $2,800,000 (milestones in 2024)  
- Less Signing Bonus (consideration payable to customer): $(8,500,000) (Dec 2024)  
- Less DTI Grant pass-through (South Africa location): $(1,547,089) over 5 years  
- Excluding optional FTE rate-card services ($8,834,318) that are invoiced only when deployed (not committed at inception)  

Computed transaction price for allocation (committed scope): $111,657,233 (= $120,491,551 total post-DTI less $8,834,318 rate-card)

**Allocation of variable consideration**

**Question:** Is there an allocation of a variable consideration ? (ASC 606-10-32-39 to 32-41)?

**Answer:** **Yes** — Variable elements are specifically attributable to particular services and are therefore allocated to the related performance obligation(s), consistent with ASC 606-10-32-40:
- SLA service credits: The “At Risk Amount” is defined as up to 10% of the invoice amount “for a specific services under a Work Order,” with credits determined by an “Allocated Percentage” per SLA in the Service Level Matrix and applied against the next invoice after earnback eligibility is assessed. Because the credits are computed from, and applied to, the invoice for the applicable services, they relate specifically to the affected service stream and are allocated to that performance obligation and period  .
- Earnbacks: The contract provides for earnbacks when the contracted Service Level is met at least two consecutive times in the six-month period following a default; true-up occurs annually. These are evaluated and recognized against the same service stream to which the underlying credits related, preserving the specific attribution to the impacted performance obligation .
- DTI grant pass-through: The DTI benefit applies to the South Africa location and is passed through “at actuals.” Because it pertains to South Africa operations, it is allocated to the Phone support performance obligation (which includes South Africa Phone) to which it specifically relates  .

In summary, fixed consideration (including technology, transition, and transformation amounts, net of the signing bonus and DTI) is allocated to the three performance obligations on a relative SSP basis using observable Fixed Service Charges from the Annexure. Variable consideration (SLA credits/earnbacks and location-specific DTI pass-through) is allocated to the specific service(s) to which it relates per the contract mechanics.
