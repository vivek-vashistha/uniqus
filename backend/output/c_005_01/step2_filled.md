# Step 2: Identifying the Performance Obligation in a Contract

The process of identifying performance obligations requires determining whether promised goods or services are distinct.

## Analyzing whether Identified Promises Represent Performance Obligations

*Guidance*

A promise is classified as a performance obligation if the good or service is capable of being distinct and distinct in the context of the contract.

### Criterion 1
Can the customer benefit from the goods or services on its own or with other readily available resources?

### Criterion 2
Is the company's promise to transfer the good or service separately identifiable from other promises in the contract?
